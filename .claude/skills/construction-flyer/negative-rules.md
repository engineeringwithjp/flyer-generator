# Negative Rules: Anti-Patterns & Prohibited Elements

## 1. Universal Prohibitions (Zero Tolerance)
The following elements must NEVER appear in any flyer generated for All Elite Construction:

1. **Em Dashes (`—` or `--`)**: Strictly forbidden. Use clean periods, bullet points (`•`), or pipes (`|`).
2. **AI / Marketing Emojis**: Never include `🔥`, `🚀`, `⭐`, `💥`, `🏠`, or `🔨`.
3. **Fabricated Claims**: Never invent discounts ("$1,000 OFF"), warranties ("Lifetime guarantee"), or fake awards unless specified in `client.json`.
4. **Surreal Architecture**: No warped windows, impossible roof pitches, melted gutters, or non-Northeast suburban houses.
5. **Cheesy Contractor Tropes**:
   - No cartoon mascots or thumbs-up stock contractors in pristine hard hats pointing at clipboards.
   - No yellow and black caution tape graphics.
   - No giant "SALE!" starburst explosion badges.

---

## 2. Layout & Design Anti-Patterns
1. **Canva Clutter**: Do not scatter 8 tiny floating card elements across the canvas.
2. **Corporate Infographics**: Flyers are emotional and visual advertisements, not corporate slide decks.
3. **Over-Branding**: Do not flood the background with crimson `#80272B`. It is an accent, not wallpaper.
4. **Text Over Important Architecture**: Never slap a large opaque text block directly over the front door, porch, or roofline being showcased.
5. **Low Contrast Typography**: White text without a dark underlay against a bright sky or white siding is an immediate failure.
