# Residential construction marketing

## Who you are talking to

A homeowner, 35-70, scrolling Facebook or Instagram on a phone. They are not
shopping for a roof. They are being reminded that they have been ignoring one.

They are cautious for good reasons: they have heard the storm-chaser stories,
they cannot evaluate workmanship, and the purchase is four to five figures.
Everything in the flyer either builds or spends trust.

What actually moves them:

- **Recognition.** A symptom they can see on their own house today.
- **Proof.** Real work, real neighbourhood, real company.
- **Low-commitment next step.** An inspection or estimate, not a purchase.
- **Local specificity.** "New Jersey homeowners" beats "homeowners".

What loses them:

- Stock-photo perfection with no company identity
- Discount language with no credibility behind it
- Anything that reads as a national lead-aggregator advert
- Claims the flyer cannot back up

## Campaign angles

| Angle | What it does | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `upgrade` | Frames work as a choice, not a disaster | Can feel optional; pair with a reason to act |
| `problem` | Names a visible symptom | Must be a real, checkable symptom |
| `urgency` | Time-bound reason to act now | Never fabricate a storm or a deadline |
| `offer` | Leads with an authorised promotion | Only from `client.offers` |
| `proof` | Before/after, completed work | Needs real photography to land |
| `education` | Teaches one useful thing | Keep it to one thing |
| `emotional` | Family, home, protection | Restrained. Never saccharine |
| `premium` | Quiet confidence, minimal copy | No discount language at all |
| `trust` | Licensing, insurance, tenure | Only what `proof_points` states |

## Seasonality (Northeast US / New Jersey)

| Season | Leads with | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring (Mar-May) | Post-winter inspection, storm damage, curb appeal, siding | Heating |
| Summer (Jun-Aug) | Roof replacement, siding, windows, energy efficiency, AC | Snow/ice |
| Fall (Sep-Nov) | Winter prep, gutters and guards, heating, last-call replacement | New large exterior builds late in the season |
| Winter (Dec-Feb) | Emergency repair, ice dams, interior remodelling, planning/financing | Full roof replacement urgency |

## Service-specific notes

**Roofing.** The largest ticket and the most competitive category. Missing
shingles, granules in the gutter, ceiling stains and daylight in the attic are
symptoms homeowners recognise. Age (20+ years) is the strongest non-damage hook.

**Siding.** Sells on appearance and energy, not failure. Before/after is the
single most effective format. Curb appeal and resale are legitimate motivators.

**Gutters.** Small ticket, high volume, strongly seasonal. Guards sell on
never-climb-a-ladder-again. Overflow, fascia rot and basement water are the
symptoms.

**Windows.** Sells on comfort, draughts, noise and bills. Energy claims must be
generic unless the client's `proof_points` back a specific number.

**HVAC / plumbing / electrical.** Emergency-driven. Response time and
availability matter more than price. "Same day" only if the client says so.

**Remodelling.** Aspirational and long-cycle. Before/after and finished-room
photography, low text, no discount language.

## Language that reads as a scam

Never use, in any form:

- "Free roof", "no cost to you", "insurance will pay for everything"
- "Guaranteed approval", "we'll get your claim approved"
- "Act now — only 3 spots left" (unless the client genuinely runs it)
- "#1 rated" / "best in New Jersey" without a citable source
- Fake countdowns, fake scarcity, fake urgency

Contractors get reported for these. The system must not generate them.
