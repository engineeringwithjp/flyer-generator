# Negative rules — the NEVER / AVOID list

The machine-readable source is `design-system/failures/failed-patterns.json`.
Everything here is a failure condition, not a preference.

## Copy

- Excessive text. More than roughly 25 words on the flyer.
- Repeating the same information in two places.
- Any invented statistic, discount, warranty, certification, award, customer
  count, tenure or licensing claim.
- Free-roof / insurance-approval / guaranteed-claim language.
- Fabricated scarcity, countdowns or storm urgency.
- Generic AI marketing filler (see `copywriting-rules.md`).

## Layout

- Huge banners and oversized colour bars swallowing the photograph.
- Repetitive card grids — three identical boxes of copy.
- Corporate infographic layouts. This is an advert, not a slide.
- Rigid template symmetry that reads as stock.
- Over-designed compositions. Restraint beats decoration.
- Critical content against an edge.

## Photography

- Fake-looking or AI-looking houses.
- Exaggerated mansions, fantasy architecture, impossible roof geometry.
- Inaccurate construction detail — wrong course spacing, impossible flashing,
  gutters attached to nothing.
- Excessive HDR, artificial lighting, architectural distortion.
- Unrealistic landscaping.
- Redesigning a real property instead of cropping it.

## Colour

- Excessive gradients.
- Flooding large areas with the brand colour.
- More than one accent colour.
- Any text below its contrast floor.

## Typography

- Microscopic text.
- More than two type families.
- Muddy size steps — two levels that are nearly the same size.
- Everything emphasised, therefore nothing emphasised.
- Paragraph blocks.

## Iconography

- Icons used as decoration for empty space.
- Unrelated, oversized or stylistically inconsistent icons.
- More than three icons.

## Branding

- A manufacturer brand leading the flyer instead of All Elite.
- A manufacturer logo dominating.
- Reformatted or "improved" contact details.

## Overall quality

If the flyer resembles any of these, regenerate:

generic AI advertisement · Canva template · corporate infographic ·
random AI poster · over-symmetrical template · over-designed advertisement
