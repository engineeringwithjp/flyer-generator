# Branding rules

## All Elite is the advertiser

The client is the brand on the flyer. Everything else is subordinate.

## Manufacturer products

Products such as GAF Timberline HDZ, ASCEND® Composite Foam-Backed Siding and
CertainTeed® CertaPlank® are **material options the client installs**. They are
never the advertising brand.

**Allowed**

- In the supporting line: "ASCEND® composite siding with built-in insulation."
- As a benefit bullet: "GAF Timberline HDZ system"
- As a credential, when it is in `proof_points`: "GAF Master Elite contractor"

**Not allowed**

- A manufacturer name as the headline
- A manufacturer logo larger than or more prominent than the client's
- Copy that reads as the manufacturer's advert rather than the contractor's
- Any manufacturer performance claim not listed in that product's `benefits`

## Restraint

The logo sits in a corner at modest scale. Contact details are legible but are
never the hero. A flyer whose most prominent element is a phone number has
failed at everything before it.

## Colour

The brand colour is punctuation, not paint. Use the accent for the CTA, an
accent word, a rule, a badge, a bullet marker. Do not flood large areas with it.

Where the property photograph has its own strong palette, let that lead and let
the brand colour punctuate.

## Contact details

Copied byte-for-byte from `client.json`. Never reformatted, abbreviated,
prettified or "corrected". A wrong phone number is the only truly unrecoverable
flyer defect.

## Tone

Professional, trustworthy, premium, modern, architectural, homeowner-focused.

Never invent brand claims. If a positioning statement is not in `client.json`,
it does not exist.
