# Branding Rules: Restraint & Manufacturer Hierarchy

## 1. Client Identity: All Elite Construction Corp.
The contractor is the hero brand and the contractual party the homeowner trusts. All branding must be clear, authoritative, and restrained.

- **Primary Logo Placement**: Top-left or top-center in clean architectural badge format, or anchored in the bottom branding footer.
- **Brand Color Allocation**:
  - **`#80272B` (Accent Crimson)**: Maximum 10-15% of canvas area. Reserved for CTA button, pill tags, key words, and micro-dividers.
  - **`#1C1C1E` / `#000000` (Dark Neutrals)**: Text, dark card backdrops, gradient overlays.
  - **`#FFFFFF` / `#F4F4F6` (Light Neutrals)**: Headlines on dark backgrounds, clean card backgrounds.

---

## 2. Manufacturer Branding Subordination
Contractors install elite products from major building material manufacturers (e.g. Alside ASCEND®, CertainTeed® CertaPlank®, GAF Timberline®, Owens Corning Duration®).

**Strict Hierarchy**:
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PRIMARY BRAND: All Elite Construction  │
│ (The installing contractor & warranty) │
└───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                    ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PRODUCT OPTION: ASCEND® Composite      │
│ (The material chosen for the project)  │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Manufacturer logos must **never** overshadow All Elite's company name.
- Present products as:
  - "Installed by All Elite Construction"
  - "Featuring ASCEND® Composite Siding Technology"
  - "Authorized CertainTeed® Installer"
- Never let the flyer look like a corporate shingle brochure or supplier mailer.
